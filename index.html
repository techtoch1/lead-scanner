DNS lookups run from your browser through Google Public DNS (Cloudflare as fallback). Nothing you enter is stored anywhere. "Review" means the domain sits behind an email security gateway and its SPF record doesn't show which mailbox provider is behind it. Email security: At risk means no DMARC or SPF (or a broken one), Weak means records exist but don't block spoofed mail (DMARC p=none, SPF ?all), Good means SPF plus an enforced DMARC policy. DKIM is checked on common selectors only, so "not found" isn't proof it's missing.
